news

Papers, talks, awards and travel.

Jun 05, 2026 I was awarded the Robert O. Pepin Fellowship by the University of Minnesota to support my graduate research over the summer.
Apr 28, 2026 I spent April 2026 in Italy as a visiting scholar at the Italian National Institute for Astrophysics (INAF), supported by the ACME Transnational Access program. While there, I worked on machine learning classifiers—including Random Forest and XGBoost models—and alert streaming tools using the Babamul Kafka consumer for rapid transient follow-up.
Oct 18, 2025 I gave a contributed talk at the AstroRob VIII workshop on autonomous robotic observatories. I spoke about our work modernizing NASA’s General Coordinates Network (GCN) to support rapid alert distribution for time-domain and multi-messenger astronomy.
Jul 22, 2025 My paper, IIb or not IIb: A Catalog of ZTF Kilonova Imposters, was accepted for publication by PASP. In this work, we present a catalog of Type IIb supernovae and other fast-evolving transients that can mimic kilonova light curves in wide-field surveys.
Jan 14, 2025 I attended the 245th AAS meeting in National Harbor, Maryland, where I presented a poster on identifying Type IIb supernovae and other kilonova imposters in ZTF data.
May 15, 2024 I attended the Hot Wiring the Transient Universe VII workshop in Toronto, Canada. I gave my first contributed talk, where I discussed the use of the Multi-Armed Bandit algorithm in selecting fast transients for follow-up observation.
May 07, 2024 I completed the Data Science in Multi-Messenger Astronomy (DSMMA) program at UMN. I spoke about the use of a Multi-Armed Bandit algorithm in searching for kilonova candidates in wide field surveys, particularly ZTF.
Apr 20, 2024 My first paper as first author was accepted for publication by MNRAS: An online framework for fitting fast transient light curves.
Mar 24, 2024 I spent most of March 2024 in Germany, working with NMMA collaborators at the University of Potsdam, followed by a weeklong workshop in Wismar. The workshop was a great opportunity to meet with the NMMA team and discuss the future of the project. I also had the chance to explore Europe for the first time. I’m looking forward to continuing our work on NMMA, and I’d love to make it back to Germany soon!
Jan 10, 2024 I presented a poster on my work with NASA’s General Coordinates Network (GCN) at the 243rd AAS meeting in New Orleans. I also helped with the GCN Booth throughout the week as an exhibitor.
Dec 06, 2023 I successfully passed my preliminary oral exam, progressing into my PhD candidacy phase.
Oct 11, 2023 I won the Departmental 3 Minute Thesis Competition again! I presented an updated look at the online framework for fitting fast transients
Oct 07, 2022 I won the Departmental 3 Minute Thesis Competition! I presented my work on the nmma fitting pipeline.
