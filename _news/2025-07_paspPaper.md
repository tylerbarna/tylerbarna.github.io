My paper, [IIb or not IIb: A Catalog of ZTF Kilonova Imposters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15900), was accepted for publication by PASP. In this work, we present a catalog of Type IIb supernovae and other fast-evolving transients that can mimic kilonova light curves in wide-field surveys.
